All along Or’Wid the refineries woke, their great engines creaking like old men rising from their beds. Their furnaces fired and chimneys beginning their daily exhale of billowing vapor. He watched as the ragged domers emerged from their tenements and joined a growing throng of hangdog shufflers, all heading to one miserable purpose or another.

Overhead metal squealed and chains chattered as the crane arms were extended out over the streets in preparation for the loading and unloading of the Magnium ore.
