Despite the urgency in the Doctor’s step and Treaky’s own macabre excitement at being called on to investigate a murder, he found himself taken in once again by the structure. The clean height seemed to defy the dim and hazy confines as it rose up and through the Sliver. Forgetting himself, Treaky stopped to crane his neck in a vain attempt to take in the Tower in its entirety.

The looming structure grew before them as they came closer and closer, and Treaky’s familiar sense of fascination with the smooth white-walled giant began to shift to nervous apprehension. Current, the ancient magic that built this tower, was the same power that had brought the skies down. Did he really want to get closer to something that was built from that power?
