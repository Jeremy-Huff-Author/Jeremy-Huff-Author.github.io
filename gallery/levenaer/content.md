Levenaer floated, held to the great tower by a chain of unimaginable size.

All along the tops of its walls were towers, walks, cranes, and scaffolded aerdocks. Tied to the docks on chains of their own were great aerships, like sleeping beasts, hovering around the floating city.

And still they climbed, until he could see down into Levenaer, and see the descending terraces and the many small buildings and parks and fountains and tiny lights burning in a city so unlike the one they had just left behind.
