The four men stepped up to the vapor gate. 

The large orifice was set into the side of the great dome’s wall, its bulky metal construction appearing crude and angular next to the smooth sweep of the dome’s mysterious surface. Up and down the sides of the vapor gate were the pipes and tubing that allowed for the distribution of the pressurized air that, once activated and opened, would hold at bay the noxious gases of the planet’s surface.

The small party stepped into the haze of vapor that swirled within the confines of the opening.
