Even midday in Levendom was as dusk, but it was always night in the Twix.

The warren of tight streets and tall looming buildings conspired to keep even the meager lights of the dome from penetrating down to the rough cobbles. Pools of tinted lights made beacons in the dimness, their colored shades each a code for those who knew their meaning. Sally pointed these out as they wound their way deeper into the darkness: Green, a breath den; Blue, a tap house. The sick yellow was for gambling, and the occasional tallow candles marked the home of an alley witch, who might read your fortune or curse your neighbor, either, for a bit or two.
