### “My kingdom for half a goose” (100 wds or fewer)



"Are you certain?"

I cradled my bloody hand to my chest.

"Ate it? The whole thing?"

"Quite certain, my Lady!"

That goose has a devil in him!

Before me, the fat proud gander paraded, every bobbing strut a taunt.

"Let's see it then," Lady Fell said, voice straining to hold both concern and disgust.

Breathing deeply, I extended my hand. There, where once my royal signet had been, was a bloody nub.

"Oh! Your ring! Your majesty, what will you do?"

I set my wounded hand to the haft of my sword.

"I am going to get my ring back."
