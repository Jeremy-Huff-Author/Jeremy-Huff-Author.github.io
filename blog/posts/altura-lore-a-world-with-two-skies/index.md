One of the most fundamental and striking features of the fictional planet of Altura is its bifurcated atmosphere. The air of Altura is divided into two vertically layered bodies, one upper and one lower. The lower atmosphere is the native atmosphere of Altura, a thick and cloudy nitros oxide. The upper atmosphere is a nitrogen oxygen mix similar to our atmosphere on earth. 

This state of division cannot be explained through natural processes. It is, instead, the result of a massive terraformation effort that ended in catastrophic failure.

## Origins of the Bifurcation

The bifurcated atmosphere resulted from the catastrophic collapse of Altura’s nanofiber world house during the Skyfall epoch. This structure was originally built by the colonists to enclose and terraform the planet — it acted as an artificial sky, holding in a breathable nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere while keeping the native nitrous oxide-rich air out.

When the world house failed:

The lighter O₂/N₂ mix that had been trapped beneath the world house floated upward.

The heavier native N₂O atmosphere sank downward, filling the lowlands, valleys, and surface depressions.

This created a long-lasting atmospheric inversion, where gases separated into distinct vertical layers due to their relative molecular weights and densities:

Nitrous oxide (N₂O, molecular weight ~44) is heavier than the oxygen-nitrogen mix (~29 average molecular weight).

Gravity caused the denser gas to settle closer to the surface, while the lighter gas rose.

## Structure of the Atmosphere Today

After Skyfall stabilized, Altura’s atmosphere became vertically stratified:

- Lower Layer: A dense, toxic blanket of nitrous oxide-rich "soup" that hugs the terrain, filling lowlands and enveloping the abandoned surface. This layer is lethal to unprotected oxygen-breathing humans and forms an impenetrable barrier to those in the domes.
- Upper Layer: A remnant bubble of oxygenated air clings to the upper atmosphere — above the towers, surrounding the Aerlands (floating cities tethered to the towers). This is where the sky dwellers live, enjoying breathable air and open skies.
## Social and Ecological Impact

The bifurcated atmosphere directly shapes Alturan civilization:

- Domers: Confined to sealed domes on the surface, these people labor in harsh, polluted conditions with limited air, light, and resources.
- Aerlanders: Inhabitants of the Aerlands who live above the toxic mists, in clean air, with sunlight and privilege.
- Andraforms (Mist Wraiths): A faction of humans who adapted biologically to the toxic lower atmosphere via symbiotic microbes, becoming a quasi-mythical species that can survive in the soup.
Ecologically, the split creates two distinct environments:

- The surface has largely reverted to native conditions, supporting the native Scleropneuma coral-producing microorganisms.
- The upper layer sustains Earth-origin biospheres, including human agriculture and livestock in Aerlands.

- Relative gas densities: The toxic lower atmosphere, rich in nitrous oxide (N₂O), is significantly denser than the upper nitrogen-oxygen layer, naturally maintaining stratification under gravity.
