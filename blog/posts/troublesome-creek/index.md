The Other Side of Trouble (100 wds or fewer)



"We make the south bank of the 'Rado, then we're clear--of the ranch, of Dan, his sons, and clear of his damn daughter."

I mutter to Sam, who doesn't say much, but seems to agree in principle.

Hundred yards to the river, some wet and cold, and we head on to Denver. Leave this business at the ranch behind--it'd gone sideways fast.

The only thing: Dan and the boys--all six--are skulking out all along this side of Troublesome Creek.

Nothin' for it, but that I put my spur to Sam's ribs, and we head through Troublesome for the other side.
