The novel I am currently working on is intended to be able to work both as a stand-alone, but is also the first installment in a planned trilogy.

The trilogy is intended to follow the story of a young man, Patricus "Treaky" Botmer, from humble beginnings in the slums of a domed city, up the great tower, and beyond into his exploits in the skies of Altura. In addition to Treaky, these stories will tell the story of his sister, Sabatha, as she remains in the dome and comes to realize her own incredible potential to rise above the circumstances of her birth.

The stories are intended to present a dialectic development from the claustrophobic domed existence, to the endless freedom of the Alturan skies, and back again to resolve the conflict between these two opposing perspectives.
