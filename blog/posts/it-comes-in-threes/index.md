The novel I am currenlty working on is intended to be able to work both as a stand-alone, but is also the first installment in a planned trillogy.

The trillogy is intended to follow the story of a young man, Patricus "Treaky" Botmer, from humble beginings in the slumns of a domed city, up the great tower, and beyond into his exploits in skies of Altura. In addition to Treaky, these stories will tell the story of his sister, Sabatha, as she remains in the dome and comes to realize her own incredible potential to rise above the circumstances of her birth.

The stories are intended to present a dialectic development from the clausterphobic domed existence, to the endless freedom of the Alturan skies, and back again to resolve the conflict between these two oposing perspectives. I played around a lot with the naming scheme for these stories. Though I have not settled fully on any particular name, here are the ones I am currently leaning towards:
