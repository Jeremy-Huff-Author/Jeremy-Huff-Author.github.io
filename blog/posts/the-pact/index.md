### “Says the devil herself” (100 wds or fewer)



"... all that I have, all that I am, through this life, and any to come."

I speak into the circle of candles, mouth bone dry by the end.

Silence.

Smells of incense, burning hair, and the tangy scent of blood turn my stomach.

Carnage! The price I've paid for this... and... silence?

A booming voice shakes everything--tears through my mind, knocking me over.

"DONE!"

I rise, bruised, and look to the circle of candles, now an inferno of blackness and fire.

"ASK." The voice shatters my reality again.

I scream, "Who killed Ellen?"

"SHE DID IT," says the Devil, "HERSELF."
