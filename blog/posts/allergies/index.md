"Well, that didn't go to plan," Susan said, trying to hold back the disgust as she peered into the contraption.

She had never liked Scott, but that was no way to go.

"Don't touch anything!" Bernard screamed as he ran to her.

"It didn't work, Bernard! He's gone! We just vaporized our colleague..." Susan stared, disbelieving, at the pile of dust that had once been the pompous Dr. Scott Benchly.

Bernard arrived, puffing beside her.

"Don't move a muscle." He stooped low to look at the pile of matter.

"From just a single grain, I can--GRRRSHOOOSH!"

"Bless you," said Susan.
