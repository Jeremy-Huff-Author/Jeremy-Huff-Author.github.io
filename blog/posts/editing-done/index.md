I happy to announce that I have competed my first round of edits on Under the Dome Sky.



I did a two pass edit:

1. One pass to identify all the things I wanted to add/remove/change.
1. A second pass to implement each edit I identified in the first pass


I was editing for the following types of changes:

1. Structural (story-logic, arcs, stakes, promises/payoffs)
1. World & Thematics (world building, genre cohesion, thematic clarity)
1. Scene-Level (line feedback, pacing, POV, character voice)
1. Copy & Polish (syntax, proofing, formatting)


It took me about a month all together. My next step is to try and get some beta reader feedback.
