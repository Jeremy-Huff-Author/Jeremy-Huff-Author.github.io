"It's vacation, honey! A new year! We should do something crazy in 2026. We should rent a..." The elderly man scrunched up his face. "Oh! What's the word?"

His wife, who had stopped listening to him years ago, muttered under her breath, "antediluvian."

"What? No! Damn, my lethologica."

"And you remember that word?"

Reaching, snagging the word from the air, he shouted in the crowded restaurant, "Penny-Farthing!"

"Excuse me?"

"We should rent one of those odd bikes tomorrow and--"

"Can't. We're taking the grandkids to pet Susan’s goat."

He hung his head, shot down again.
